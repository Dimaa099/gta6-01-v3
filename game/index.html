Bahía Palma · a getaway-driver story

Marco Reyes, getaway driver for Carmen "La Jefa" Vega, works three jobs across the sun-soaked bay city of Bahía Palma — then the city is his.

On foot: WASD + mouse (click the game to lock the mouse), Shift sprint, E interact, click to shoot the pistol, F melee, R reload.

Driving: E to enter any car, WASD drive, Space handbrake, H horn, E to get out. At a blue race ring, E starts the race instead.

Heat: crimes raise your stars (top-left). Police pursue; if they pin you while you are slow, the BUSTED meter fills. Lose them to cool off. Death sends you to the hospital (−10% cash); arrest costs a fine. Save at Vega's Garage, from the pause menu, or automatically after each job.

Side money: green packages +$75, street races +$150 (Bay Loop, Downtown Dash, Grid Iron).

An original game. Fictional city, people and story. Built with three.js.
